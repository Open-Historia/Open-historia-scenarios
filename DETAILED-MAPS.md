# Detailed maps

Large, sharp terrain maps that scenarios are drawn on. In the game: **Maps → Community → Detailed maps**. A scenario that uses one also has its own basemap, for players who don't download it.

To share one, make a [detailed map post](https://github.com/Open-Historia/Open-historia-scenarios/issues/new?template=detailed-map.yml). This page is written by the **Detailed maps** workflow; don't edit it by hand.
